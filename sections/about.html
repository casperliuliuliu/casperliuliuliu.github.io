About Me

M.S. AI @ NCKU MMCV Lab | Research Student

Hello there!

I'm Liu Shih-Wen 劉世文 (Casper). I'm a research student in the Multimedia & Computer Vision Lab at National Cheng Kung University, mentored by Prof. Wei-Ta Chu. My master's research focuses on efficient multi task learning and multimodal large language models. I have published one CVPR paper and one MIDL paper, both in collaboration with NVIDIA Research Taiwan. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from National Sun Yat-sen University in 2024.

My core research interest is Scalable Collaborative Intelligence, building AI systems that can actively collaborate with humans, other agents, and the physical world to accelerate scientific discovery.

I am driven by the vision of AI as a true research partner, systems that can reason over multimodal information, identify what matters, organize knowledge over time, formulate ideas, and support complex research workflows. I am especially interested in scalable collaboration among multimodal large language models, humans, and robots, where each agent can focus on the most useful information and complement the others' strengths to solve problems beyond what any single intelligence can achieve alone.

This summer, I will join a research visit at BAIR and fall later another research visit at the University of Toronto, as I prepare for my Fall '27 PhD applications. You can view my CV here.
