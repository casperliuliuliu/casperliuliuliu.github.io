About Me

M.S. AI @ NCKU MMCV Lab | Research Student

Hello there!

I'm Liu Shih-Wen 劉世文 (Casper). I'm a research student in the Multimedia & Computer Vision Lab at National Cheng Kung University, mentored by Prof. Wei-Ta Chu. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from National Sun Yat-sen University in 2024. I worked on two graduate research projects under Prof. Shu-Min Li and Prof. Wen-Hsiang Lu.

My core interests are cooperative and scalable robotic intelligence. I particularly focus on designing learning mechanisms that enable multiple robots to coordinate and collaborate efficiently, especially in multi-agent robotic manipulation and control. Previously, I worked on parameter-efficient fine-tuning for multi-task learning, and histopathology report generation.

This summer, I plan to tour U.S. campuses as I prepare my Fall '27 PhD applications in cooperative and scalable robotics. You can view my CV here.
