How I stop procrastinating

Published: March 26, 2026

I used to procrastinate a lot and had a really hard time focusing. I kept trying different productivity techniques, but eventually I realized something important:

It's not about “being productive.”

It's about actually doing the thing you want to do.

So instead of finding one perfect method, I stack multiple small ones.

Each one only helps a little — but together, they push me past the tipping point into real discipline.

Here are the ones that work for me:

  • Fake rival
    Imagine someone competing with you. What would they be doing right now?
  • Say it before you do it
    Before starting, say out loud exactly what you're about to do.
  • Start with cognitively demanding tasks
    Don't warm up — go straight into the hard stuff.
  • Accountability
    Report your progress to someone (partner, friend). It works.
  • Time box + reward
    Set a time → finish → reward yourself → go for the next.
  • Short time chunks
    Every 30 minutes = new action. Don't drift for an hour.
  • Strong emotional motive
    Mine? “I want to deserve an amazing wife.”
  • Challenge mindset
    Treat work like a game you want to win.
  • Remember why you started
    Emotion > logic.
  • Be your ideal self
    If you were already that person, what would you do right now?
  • Forgive yourself fast
    Even if you wasted 3 hours or 3 days — it's fine. Restart small.
  • Just start 5 minutes
    Thinking gives questions. Doing gives answers.
  • Environment design
    Slightly public, no friends, no distractions, phone away, write things down, say your task, start timer.
  • Keep moving (write/type constantly)
    Action keeps momentum alive.
  • Start by talking to ChatGPT
    This helps you break inertia.
