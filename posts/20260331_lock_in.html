The Momentum to Lock In

Published: March 31, 2026

It's not easy to "lock in."

People often think you need the perfect state—high energy, zero distractions, the right mood, everything aligned.

But that's not true.

You don't prepare to lock in.

You enter it by starting.

Think of “lock in” like your brain reaching a high processing speed.

Yes, good conditions help—you accelerate faster when you're rested and focused.

But speed doesn't come first.

Motion does.

Like running, you can't go from 0 → 100 in one second. That's unrealistic.

Instead, you build up speed:

0 → 60 → 80 → 90 → 95

Action creates momentum.

Momentum creates lock in.

So don't wait to feel ready.

Just start, and let the momentum catch up to you.
